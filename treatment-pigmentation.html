ABOUT THIS CONCERN

Understand the concern.
Then choose the next step.

Pigmentation can appear as uneven tone, patches or darker areas that develop for different reasons. A consultation helps separate what you are noticing from the approach that may be appropriate for your skin.

At Visage, the discussion is designed to stay clear and individual. The clinic can explain what is relevant to your concern, what may be appropriate and what should be considered before moving forward.

WHAT YOU MAY NOTICE
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Dark patches or areas of discolouration
  • Post-inflammatory darkening
  • Changes that appear after sun exposure or irritation
WHAT MAY BE DISCUSSED
  • Where and when the pigmentation appeared
  • Your history of sensitivity and sun exposure
  • Current skincare and previous treatments
  • Options that may suit your individual skin and goals

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Come prepared.

Bring photographs only if they genuinely help show how the concern has changed over time. Also share any products that have previously irritated your skin.

AFTER YOUR VISIT

Keep the plan clear.

Pigmentation discussions often include guidance around consistent skincare and sun protection. Follow the clinic’s personalised advice.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Helpful things
to know.

Is all pigmentation the same?

No. Different patterns can have different causes and should not be treated as one identical concern.

Can I discuss sensitive skin as well?

Yes. Skin sensitivity is relevant when considering what may be suitable.

Will I receive a treatment plan immediately?

The next step depends on the consultation and what the clinic considers appropriate.
