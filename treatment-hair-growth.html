ABOUT THIS CONCERN

Understand the concern.
Then choose the next step.

Hair growth therapies are discussed after understanding the pattern of thinning, shedding and scalp health. The right approach depends on the individual concern rather than on a single universal treatment.

At Visage, the discussion is designed to stay clear and individual. The clinic can explain what is relevant to your concern, what may be appropriate and what should be considered before moving forward.

WHAT YOU MAY NOTICE
  • A desire to support visible hair density
  • Early or ongoing thinning
  • Questions about clinic-led growth therapies
  • A wish to understand maintenance options
WHAT MAY BE DISCUSSED
  • Your pattern of hair loss or thinning
  • Current scalp condition and hair routine
  • What therapy options may be relevant
  • How progress and follow-up could be planned

COMMON QUESTIONS

Helpful things
to know.

Will one therapy suit everyone?

No. The approach depends on the individual pattern and assessment.

How is progress assessed?

The clinic may discuss consistent review and documentation rather than relying on day-to-day impressions.

Can this be discussed with hair transplant options?

Yes. If relevant, the consultation can compare appropriate next steps.
