ABOUT THIS CONCERN

Understand the concern.
Then choose the next step.

Hair fall and hair loss can look similar while having different patterns and histories. The consultation starts by understanding what you have noticed and what information is needed before discussing the next step.

At Visage, the discussion is designed to stay clear and individual. The clinic can explain what is relevant to your concern, what may be appropriate and what should be considered before moving forward.

WHAT YOU MAY NOTICE
  • Increased shedding
  • Visible thinning or widening of a parting
  • Changes in hair density over time
  • A sudden or recurring pattern you want to discuss
WHAT MAY BE DISCUSSED
  • When the concern started and how it has changed
  • Scalp symptoms and hair-care habits
  • Relevant medical or family history where appropriate
  • Whether further assessment or a clinic-led plan is needed

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Come prepared.

Bring a brief timeline of when you first noticed the change. Avoid making several major routine changes immediately before the consultation if you want to describe your usual pattern.

AFTER YOUR VISIT

Keep the plan clear.

Any treatment, testing or follow-up recommendation should be based on the individual assessment.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Helpful things
to know.

Is all hair fall the same?

No. Shedding and progressive thinning can have different patterns and require different discussions.

Can scalp health be discussed too?

Yes. The scalp and hair are closely connected in the consultation.

Will I need a long-term plan?

That depends on the pattern identified and the goals discussed with the clinic.
